# Data sources and claim boundaries

## Experimental metallic-contact benchmark

The measured stainless-steel friction loops are taken from Fantetti, Botto, Schwingshackl and Zucca's 2024 public dataset:

- Article: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2024.110374
- Source data: https://doi.org/10.17632/gy587m7gx7.1

The registered nine terminal-loop conditions and three time-history conditions are chosen from **the same measurement archive**. Repeated cycles from one experimental condition are dependent observations, not separately recruited experiments.

Raw `.mat` measurement files and historical copied illustrations are **not present** in this public repository. Reusers should obtain original data from its provider and comply with its applicable license. Exact SHA-256 source manifests accompany the code-only release candidate but are not a substitute for source files.

## Historical pneumatic illustration

The 600-point pneumatic friction trajectory was digitized from a figure in Recchia's thesis attributed to the Nouri (2004) experiment. It is not Nouri's original sensor acquisition and is not distributed in this public repository.

## Data availability

Full reproduction requires obtaining the relevant original data from the official archive and separately securing an authorized copy of any historical figure digitization. Results must not be characterized as independent pneumatic-seal validation.
